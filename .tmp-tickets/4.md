## What to build

When a trip's end date has passed, the trip shows a recap: where the group went, how many days, what it cost in total and per person, and what is still owed. From it a member can settle up, share a summary card, and start the next trip from this one.

This is aimed at the second thing Floc still has to prove: that a group comes back. The share card is also a way new people meet Floc.

There is no lifecycle state (rule 4): the recap comes from the data — an end date in the past — never from a status column. A trip with no dates (rule 9) has no recap and that is not an error.

## Decide in grilling

- What the recap holds, and where it lives: the overview, its own view, or a notification.
- The share card: what it shows to people outside the trip. No money, no member names beyond what the group agrees.
- "Plan the next one": how it meets clone and templates (#335).
- Whether a notification or email announces the recap (no marketing email in v1).
- Photos are out of this ticket (#167).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A trip whose end date has passed shows a recap; an undated or future trip does not
- [ ] The recap shows route, days, total cost and each member's balance from existing data, with money in minor units through @floc/core/money
- [ ] Open balances link to settling up
- [ ] A member can share a summary card that holds nothing private to the group
- [ ] A member can start a new trip from the recap
- [ ] Present on web and phone, or the gap declared in parity.json with a why
