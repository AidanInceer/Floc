## What to build

A section on the landing page that shows what real groups say about Floc. Real words from real people only, never invented or placeholder quotes (Honesty and openness; honest proof, ADR-019). Layout starts from the front-door mockup pick, reviews D.

## Decide before the build

- Where the reviews come from: the in-app feedback form (#271), a direct ask to the first groups, or store reviews once the app is out.
- Consent: each person agrees in writing to the quote and to how they are named.
- What the section shows while there are too few reviews: it hides, it does not fill with samples.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The landing page shows reviews from real users, each with recorded consent
- [ ] With fewer reviews than the agreed floor the section does not render
- [ ] No review carries more personal detail than its author agreed to
- [ ] Reads clearly at 1280 and 375, light and dark, tokens only
- [ ] The front-door doc page lists the section

## Blocked by

None in tickets. Needs real groups with something to say, so it can only finish once there are reviews to show.
