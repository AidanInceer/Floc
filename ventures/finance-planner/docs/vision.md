# Vision

## One-liner

Turn a household's messy, multi-account financial reality into a clear picture
of *where the money is, where it's going, and what to do next* — with as little
manual input as possible.

## The problem

Understanding personal finances today means either:

- Living inside a fragile bespoke spreadsheet (powerful, but two hours of data
  entry and easy to break), or
- Using budgeting apps that track the past but don't **forecast** or **advise**.

Finance Planner started as exactly the former: a bespoke Excel model
(`Hertford_Mill_Financial_Dashboard.xlsx`) built to answer one specific
question — *"given a house purchase, what does my cash flow and savings look
like over the next six months, and where's the lowest point?"* It works, but it
doesn't generalise and it's all manual.

## The product

Finance Planner generalises that model into a modular web app:

1. **Plug in your banks** via open banking — transactions + balances flow in
   automatically. Minimal typing.
2. **See your cash flow forecast** — known incomes, recurring outgoings, and
   upcoming one-offs projected forward; surface the lowest point and the runway.
3. **Get generalised guidance** — grounded in UK tax/benefit knowledge bases
   and configurable financial-health rubrics (emergency fund sizing, order of
   savings, steps toward financial resilience), tuned to the user's stated risk
   appetite.

## Principles

- **Minimal input, maximal insight.** Every required field is a tax on adoption.
  Default and derive aggressively; ask only what genuinely can't be inferred.
- **Modular & expandable.** New data feeds, new markets, new advice rubrics slot
  in as modules without rewrites.
- **Guidance, not regulated advice.** Informational and educational, always
  sourced. Not a substitute for a regulated adviser.
- **Trustworthy with money.** Correct arithmetic, secure by default, private.

## Who it's for (now vs later)

- **Now (Phase 0–1):** a single user (the author), self-hosted, over the next
  couple of years — to validate categorisation quality and forecasting accuracy
  against real spending.
- **Later:** multi-user, multi-market, richer advice. Only after the core earns
  trust on real data.

## Non-goals (for the MVP)

- Not a full accounting/tax-filing tool.
- Not a trading or brokerage platform.
- Not giving regulated, personalised investment advice.
- Not multi-currency portfolio management (single home currency first: GBP).
