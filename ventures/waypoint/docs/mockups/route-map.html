              Two continents

              bounds, zoomed right out

                Placeholder image box

                postage-stamp edge

                A stamp-shaped empty frame, for wherever a picture hasn’t been added yet. The perforated edge is a clip-path polygon — semicircular bites along each side, and a quarter-arc corner that sticks out exactly as far as a bite’s tip does, so the corners stay flush at any shape or size — so the white shape is genuinely cut out and sits on the cream page rather than being a white rectangle with a drawn border. Landscape, portrait and square, plus one holding a photo.

                What this prototype is arguing for

                • Treatment C, the paper wash. B is the safe fallback if the sepia reads as muddy on other screens; A is not a candidate.
                • Fixed by default, no pan or zoom. Route is a summary tab — the map answers “roughly where, in what order”, and a map you can lose your place in fights that. Toggle it above and compare.
                • Numbered pins in stop order, a dashed straight line between them. No routing API: the line is a relationship, not a driving route, and dashing it says so. Nights ride as a small tab on the pin.
                • Bounds fitted to the pins with padding, capped at zoom 12 so a single stop doesn’t land on someone’s street.
                • No coordinates, no map — a one-line notice above the unchanged stop list, never an empty grey box.
                • Attribution stays visible in every treatment (OSM tile policy). The filters never touch it.
