The whole journey, one page

Six stops between “we should go somewhere” and “that’s settled”.

Nothing here is a wizard. A coupon opens when the group is ready for it, and a trip that already knows where it’s going tears straight through to the route.

Stop 01
Ideas

Nobody can settle on where to go

Everyone adds the places they would go — a link, a photo, a name someone half-remembers — and votes on each other’s. The board shows which ideas the group actually likes, so the choice isn’t whoever argued last.

4 votes Sicily — “hear me out”
2 votes Puglia, if we can drive
no votes That place Jonas saw once
Stop 02
Dates

Finding the one week everyone can do

Each person shades in the days they’re free. Waypoint overlaps all of it and shows which runs of days work and who’s missing from each, so a date gets picked without another poll in the chat.

5 free 12–19 Sep · the one everyone can do
3 free 26 Sep–3 Oct · Tom’s away
Stop 03
Days

Where you sleep, and what happens each day

Say where the group is staying on each day — a run of days in one place is one stay, drawn as one bar — and fill in what’s planned, roughly when, and who’s holding the tickets. Days left mostly empty stay that way: this is a plan, not a schedule.

3 nights Taormina, then 2 in Syracuse
09:30 Market, then coffee at Bam Bar
20:00 Dinner, table for 7
Stop 04
Money

Working out who owes who

Everyone logs what they paid and how it was split. Waypoint nets it all off to one figure per person. It records the debt — no money moves through Waypoint.

£1,240 spent, across 14 expenses
£177 each, near enough
£84 owed to Mei by 3 people
Stop 05
After

Keeping the trip after it’s over

A finished trip is archived rather than deleted: the dates, the route, the days and the final bill stay readable, so the next trip starts from what actually happened.
