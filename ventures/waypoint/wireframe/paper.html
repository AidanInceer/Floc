Waypoint the trip book
A notebook nine people can write in at once

Start the trip as notes. Finish it as a plan.

Everyone scribbles: a place, a price, the week they can't do, the pub someone swears by. Waypoint keeps it all on one page and quietly turns it into dates, a route and a bill everyone agrees on.
