SCALE 1 : PLAN A gazetteer nine people can enter at once

Start the trip as notes. Finish it as a surveyed plan.

Everyone marks the sheet: a place, a price, the week they can't do, the pub someone swears by. Waypoint plots it, tallies it, and draws the route — dates, legs and a bill everyone agrees on.
