🆓 Free track — core planning

Ordered highest value-to-effort first. Items 1–8 turn a planning tool into something a group actually uses on the trip.

# Idea The problem it solves Effort
1 Fronted / deposit expense flag Someone fronts £400 for the house and feels awkward chasing. Flag it "fronted" → enters the ledger immediately with a "3 of 7 squared up" state. Best value-to-effort on the page. ▁ tiny
2 Packing & jobs list "Who's bringing the speaker / booking the transfer?" One flat list per trip, each line claimable by a member. Makes the product feel finished. ▁ tiny
3 Fast expense capture Nobody fills a form at the table. Amount → one tap "split with everyone here" → done. Receipt photo optional, attached later. Pure UI on the existing money model. ▁ tiny
4 One-screen "today" view The app goes quiet the moment the trip starts. A "today" screen: day-plan, tonight's address, who's arriving, one tap to log spend. Offline-readable. Strategically the retention hook. ▂ small
5 Budget before booking Groups discover they can't afford it after booking. A per-person target ("~£600 each") + a running estimate as stops are added. Visibly an estimate. ▂ small
6 Who's arriving when Seven people, five flights, all in the chat. An arrivals/departures board — times only, free-text, no flight API. Answers "who shares the transfer?". ▂ small
7 Where everyone's sleeping The route says "Taormina, 3 nights" but not which house, whose name, what door code, who's in the annexe. A lodging record on a stop + a plain room-allocation list. ▂ small
8 When the plan breaks The ferry's cancelled and seven people learn separately. Mark a day event "changed" with a one-line reason. A note, not a notification system. ▁ tiny
9 Options, not just ideas Voting fails for "which of these three houses". A comparison card: 2–4 options side by side (price pp, distance, sleeps) → vote. Free-text rows the group defines. ▃ medium
10 Commitment state The gap between "yeah I'm keen" and "I've booked" is where trips die. Per-member interested → in → booked, self-declared. Overview: "5 of 7 in, 2 booked". ▂ small
11 Search across the archive "Where was that bar in Ortigia?" is buried in last year's trip. Search across every trip you've been on. Cheap; the long-term retention story. ▂ small
12 Clone / blueprint a past trip Start a new trip from a past one (or a friend's), then customise. Pairs with the archive search above and with /explore. ▂ small
13 Durable travel group The trip is a one-off; the group isn't. Save the roster and carry settled balances forward so last year's debt isn't forgotten. Cross-trip balances are a real data-model call — decide early. ▃ medium
14 Quiet voices Two people decide everything; one hates the plan silently until day three. A light anonymous "does this work for everyone?" check. Easily creepy — research before design. ▃ medium
15 Profile page + world map A proper profile: a world map of countries visited (green) and want-to-visit (yellow), shareable with friends. ▃ medium
16 Profile picture Straightforward avatar upload. ▁ tiny
17 Interests / vibe tags — actually used The vibe-preference and interest/category tags exist but do nothing. Wire them into pre-creating trips with friends. ▂ small
18 Dietary requirements Per-member dietary flags, surfaced where the group books food. ▁ tiny
19 Parent / carer role A member who can add children to the trip, distinct from member/admin. ▂ small
20 Reminders (non-nagging) A light reminder system to aid planning without feeling like chasing. Fits "nudges, not nagging". ▂ small
21 Basic weather Near-term forecast on the trip. (AI weather-aware planning is the paid version — item P6.) ▂ small

💳 Paid track — AI, automation & premium

Bigger bets and the tier that justifies a subscription. Ordered by "magic per unit effort".

# Idea What it does Notes
P1 Import booking confirmations from email Parse flight/hotel/car confirmations from the inbox and auto-fill the trip. The strongest "this feels like magic" automation; feeds arrivals, lodging & budget for free. Privacy-heavy — scoped, explicit consent.
P2 Visa & passport checks From nationality + destination, tell each member whether they need a visa, link the source, and flag passport expiry. Free self-declared checklist in the free tier; automatic lookup is paid. Data must be current or it's a liability.
P3 AI packing recommendations Given destination, dates & travel style, generate a packing list and de-duplicate across the group (one speaker, not seven). Builds on the free packing list.
P4 AI trip summaries A readable summary of the plan / a trip recap. Low-risk, high-delight entry AI feature.
P5 Flight tracking + live delay alerts Track booked flights, notify on delays/cancellations, optionally nudge an itinerary reshuffle. Needs push — out of v1 scope.
P6 AI weather-aware planning Flag when a planned clear-sky activity hits a cloudy/wet day and suggest reordering.
P7 AI conflict resolution "Find a compromise everyone will like" across the group's stated preferences. Must augment the group's decision, not replace its legitimacy.
P8 AI schedule rebuilding Auto-rebuild the day plan when something breaks (delay, closure). Pairs with P5.
P9 AI suggestions — destinations / restaurants / activities Recommendations seeded by the group's tags and budget. See flag C1 on generated-itinerary legitimacy.
P10 AI budget optimisation Suggest where to trim to hit the per-person target from free item 5.
P11 Advanced voting Ranked-choice, preference weighting, scenario comparison, alternative-itinerary generation, budget simulations. Premium layer over the free comparison card (item 9).
P12 Premium maps Offline maps, custom pins, collaborative route editing, walking routes.
P13 Automatic syncing Calendar sync of the itinerary; auto-categorise expenses.
P14 Premium travel tools Currency tracking, airport terminal & lounge info, check-in reminders, local transport suggestions, busy-period/holiday flags. Grab-bag; cherry-pick, don't build wholesale.
P15 Driver's licence checks Like the visa check — whether your licence is valid to drive at the destination.

⚑ Contested — decide before building

Ideas that collide with the boundary set in CLAUDE.md or elsewhere. Ranked nowhere on purpose — pick a side first.

# Idea Why it's flagged
C1 AI travel agent / AI itinerary generation Generated plans have no social legitimacy — the hard problem is what seven people will agree to, not what's possible in Sicily. Keep AI as an assistant to the group's decision (P6–P10), not an itinerary vending machine.
C2 Photo journal / Polarsteps-style social sharing Flips the privacy model from "nine people" to "the internet". A private post-trip photo book is a possible middle path; the public feed is not.
C3 Shared live location The phone already does it, no planning value, privacy liability.
C4 Flight & hotel booking in-app Out of v1 scope — regulated and commission-driven. Search deep links only.

What to not build

Idea Why not
An AI itinerary generator The problem is never "what could we do in Sicily" — the internet is full of that. It's "what will these seven people agree to". Generated plans have no social legitimacy.
Attractions / POI database, reviews Someone else's, always out of date. Free-text place names plus a link out is enough.
Flight and hotel booking Regulated, commission-driven, and it changes what the product is for. Search deep links only.
A chat/messaging surface The group already has a chat and won't move. Note threads on specific decisions are the right level.
Live location sharing The phone already does it, and it's a privacy liability with no planning value.
Gamification (streaks, badges, "trip score") Wrong tone for a product whose entire pitch is a quiet, trustworthy document.
Public trip sharing / social feed A different product wearing this one's clothes. Changes the privacy model from "nine people" to "the internet".
