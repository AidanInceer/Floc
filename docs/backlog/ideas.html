High level ideas

  1. A proper user profile page.E.g., a world map with all of the countries that you've visited and an option to search and tag countries that you want to visit. These would show up as yellow. Visited countries would show up as green, and you could sort of share that with friends.
  2. Profile picture.
  3. And actual use for vibe preference stuff to help you pre-create trips with friends
  4. Concept of an AI agent which would be called a travel agent and sort of act on your behalf to some extent, which could be a paid feature
  5. Based on point 4, I'm thinking one of the things you could have is also a packing list and potentially a group packing list. Let's say you've filled in all the details: where you are going and what you're doing. The travel agent could then create for you, based off its knowledge base plus all of your information for your trip, who's recommended what you need to bring if you're packing light or heavy, etc., depending on user travel style. But then also things that potentially don't need to be brought by everyone because other people already have them Also, as a side note, the ability to check to see, obviously, if you're flying or travelling abroad, you need guaranteed things such as passport and visa, and that visa check could also potentially be done automatically. In terms of what your nationality is and where you're flying to or from, to know whether you need a visa and potentially recommend links
  6. I think that's also interesting with the note as well in the tick list thing, just to see a summarised checklist of whether everybody's got their visas sorted and whether everyone's got their flights or travel to the destination sorted.
  7. Sort of a clean ability to see what flights you and other people in your trip have booked
  8. Ability to see other friends' trips and then create trips or blueprints based off of them
  9. The general concept of a blueprint trip, which would also be sort of linked alongside partner trips and explore trips Where you could take certain elements of it and customise it afterwards
  10. For near-term trips, also have weather implemented. And then again, with the travel assistant potentially flagged people in the trip if they were planning on doing something that required it to be clear skies, for example. If it was shown to be cloudy, then that caused issues, and it flagged them to say, "Maybe you want to try doing something else or reorder your days slightly."
  11. The idea of a user being not only a member and an admin but also a parent or a carer or something along those lines, and they could essentially add children to the trip as well
  12. For the date availability stuff: potential flags. If you're going from the UK to France, let's say for a ski trip during the half-term holidays, then obviously maybe it's worth flagging that these are busy periods. \Just like general, what's it called Like different countries' holidays and busy periods for where you're travelling to
  13. Ability to have some sort of budgeting thing Alongside the Splitwise-style cost-sharing
  14. Ability to see the booked hotels in the app as well, or some sort of summary or subset of that, and how to link it Ideally, I want it to not necessarily feel like filling in a form. It should be automated or semi-automated.
  15. Similar to vibe preferences or user tags, also like interests/categories
  16. Again, similar to visa restrictions and stuff, potentially also like driver's licence stuff
  17. Future state in terms of self-promotion and marketing is the ability to share trips similar to how Polar Steps works on Instagram or Facebook or whatever. And also the ability to add photos Off the top So people can sort of look back at it as if it was like a notebook or like a slide deck. Potentially, with users' permission, it could sync the date in which a photo was taken on your phone to the days and the times in which you're doing things, so it could auto sync up like that.
  18. Dietary requirements and sort of user stuff.
  19. A useful reminder-style system, not to feel nagging but just to aid people in the planning process
  20. Strikes or delays or lost luggage and all that sort of stuff, and have it potentially auto-adjust depending on delays and things, maybe
