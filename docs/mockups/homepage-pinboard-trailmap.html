The whole journey, one page

Six stops between “we should go somewhere” and “that's settled”.

Nothing here is a wizard. A stop opens when the group is ready for it, and a trip that already knows where it's going skips straight to the route.

  1. Stop 01 Ideas

    Everyone throws places in

    A link, a photo, a name someone half-remembers. Vote when it helps the group decide; ignore the votes when it doesn't.

    4 votesSicily — “hear me out”
    2 votesPuglia, if we can drive
    no votesThat place Jonas saw once
    Ruth added 6 of these in one evening
  2. Stop 02 Dates

    The weeks people can't do

    Everyone shades in their bad weeks and the overlap surfaces itself. It never waits for a full house — five out of seven is usually the answer.

    5 free12–19 Sep · the one everyone can do
    3 free26 Sep–3 Oct · Tom's away
    nobody had to ask twice
  3. Stop 03 Route

    Put the stops in order

    Drag the favourites into a sequence and give each one its nights. Waypoint keeps the arithmetic honest so nobody plans four cities in three days.

    2 nightsCatania
    3 nightsTaormina
    2 nightsSyracuse
    7 nights, 7 accounted for
  4. Stop 04 Days

    The day-by-day fills itself in

    Each day gets the things worth doing, roughly in the order you'd do them. Half-empty days are fine — most of them should be.

    09:30Market, then coffee at Bam Bar
    13:00Isola Bella — Jonas has the tickets
    20:00Dinner, table for 7
    the rest of Thursday is deliberately blank
  5. Stop 05 Money

    Who owes who, worked out once

    Log what you paid as you go, split it evenly or exactly. At the end it's one number each — a ledger, not a payment app.

    £1,240spent, across 14 expenses
    £177each, near enough
    £84owed to Mei by 3 people
    settled on the Sunday, no spreadsheet
  6. Stop 06 After

    The book stays on the shelf

    The trip is archived, not deleted: the route, the photos of receipts and the arguments are all still there when the group plans the next one.
