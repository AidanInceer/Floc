Backbone

  • One activity log. Each change writes one row: who, what, which thing, when, which trip. Friend requests have no trip. Nobody sees the log.
  • Written where the fact changes. The server/ function writes the row in the same transaction. Web and phone both go through server/.
  • Grouped at write. Quick edits by one person to one thing become one row.

Logged—Log only

The person who made the change is never told.

Loud events

  • A reply to your comment
  • A new expense that includes you
  • A settlement with you
  • A nudge to you
  • A change to your packing claim
  • Trip dates change (every member)
  • A trip invite to you
  • A friend request to you, or yours accepted

Days added or removed, and members joining or leaving: inbox only. Not included: votes, decision deadlines. Mentions come later (#343).

Channels

  • Push — Expo Push, phone only. No web push.
  • Email — Resend, only when push cannot reach you. Never both.
  • Permission — asked when you first join or create a trip, after one line saying why. Never at launch.

Limits

  • Wait, then group. One message per person per trip.
  • Push: 1 per trip per 15 min, 6 per day. Email: 1 per trip per hour, 3 per day.
  • Over the limit is held and sent later as one summary. Never dropped.
  • Switches: push, email, reminders. Mute a trip from its … menu: stops push and email; inbox still updates. Friend requests and invites have no trip, so only the switches stop them.
  • Nudges obey the same rules.

Reminders

Driven by dates, not changes. Checked daily, sent from 08:00 UTC (the first cron run after). No timezones. Trips with no dates get none.

  • Once per person per trip. A reminder row with a unique (kind, trip, person) key is claimed first, so overlapping runs never send twice.
  • Go to the inbox. Obey trip mute and the reminders switch. Skip the limits.
  • No marketing or win-back reminders in v1.

Inbox

  • Bell in the top bar, web and app, with unread count.
  • Read when opened, on any device. Stored on the server.
  • Tap opens the exact thing. One web URL, which the app also handles.
  • Kept 30 days. 50 per page.
  • Hidden when its thing is deleted or you left the trip.

Sending

sequenceDiagram
    participant C as Railway cron service
    participant W as api/cron/push
    participant D as Turso
    participant P as Expo Push
    participant E as Resend

    C->>W: POST with CRON_SECRET
    W->>D: claim due reminders
    W->>D: claim rows past 2 min wait (5 min lease)
    W->>P: grouped push, within limits
    W->>D: claim rows past 10 min wait, no push reach
    W->>E: grouped email, within limits
  • Schedule */5 * * * * (Railway's floor). The cron runs node floc/apps/web/scripts/cron-push.mjs, which only calls the web service.
  • All state is in the database. A restart or a second instance never loses or doubles a message.
  • A lease means an overlapping run skips claimed rows, and a crashed send is tried again.
  • Order: reminders, push, email. One failing does not stop the others.
  • Email waits 10 minutes, so a thing already read in the app is not mailed.
  • A phone only gets notifications from after it registered.
  • A muted trip skips push and email. Its rows still reach the inbox.

Setup

  • Android push: google-services.json (EAS file variable GOOGLE_SERVICES_JSON) and the FCM key uploaded to EAS.
  • Links open the app when the site serves /.well-known/assetlinks.json (ANDROID_CERT_SHA256) and /.well-known/apple-app-site-association (APPLE_TEAM_ID). Without them, links open the website.
