Notifications

How a change in a trip reaches the people who need it, on web and phone, without flooding them. Decided in the grilling of #331.

flowchart LR
    Change["server/* change<br/>(expense, day, note, nudge…)"]
    Log[("activity<br/>one row per change,<br/>same transaction")]
    Rules["Rules<br/>who is affected,<br/>how loud, mute"]
    Inbox[("notification<br/>per person")]
    Cron["Railway cron<br/>every 5 minutes"]
    Push["Expo Push"]
    Email["Resend email"]
    Bell["Bell + inbox<br/>web and app"]

    Change --> Log --> Rules --> Inbox
    Inbox --> Bell
    Inbox --> Cron
    Cron -->|push on| Push
    Cron -->|no push| Email

The backbone

  • One activity log. Every change writes one row: who, what, which thing, when, and its trip — empty for friend requests, which belong to no trip. Nobody sees the log. It exists so the rules have one source of truth.
  • Written where the fact changes. Each server/ function that changes a fact writes its row in the same transaction — like freshness.ts. Web and the app API both go through server/, so both log the same.
  • Grouped at write. Rapid edits by the same person to the same thing fold into one row.

Loudness

LevelWhoReaches
Logged—The log only.
InboxOther membersInbox and bell count.
LoudPeople affected, and everyone for trip date changesInbox, bell, then push — or email if no push.

The person who made the change is never told.

Loud events

  • A reply to your comment.
  • A new expense that includes you.
  • A settlement with you.
  • A nudge to you.
  • A change to your packing claim.
  • Trip dates change, to every member.
  • Someone invites you to a trip.
  • Someone sends you a friend request, or accepts yours.

Days added or removed and members joining or leaving reach the inbox only. Not in scope: votes, decision deadlines. Mentions come later (#343).

Channels

  • Push via Expo Push Service, phone app only. No web push.
  • Email via Resend, only when the person has no app or push off. Never both for one notification.
  • Permission is asked when you first join or create a trip, after one line saying why — never at launch.

Not annoying

  • Wait, then group. A short wait; everything for one person and trip goes as one message.
  • Limits. Push: 1 per trip per 15 min, 6 per day. Email: 1 per trip per hour, 3 per day.
  • Over the limit is held and sent later as one summary — never dropped.
  • Settings. Push on/off, email on/off, reminders on/off. Mute a trip from its … menu: stops push and email, inbox and count still update. Friend requests and trip invites have no trip to mute, so only the push and email switches stop them.
  • Nudges go through the same rules. No path bypasses them.

Reminders

Not changes — dates. The cron checks once a day and sends at 08:00 UTC (no timezones; revisit when users outside Europe grow). Trips with no dates get none. Each sends once per person per trip.

ReminderWhoWhen
Your trip starts in a weekEvery member7 days before start
Your trip starts todayEvery memberStart date
You still owe Sam £40Each person who owesOnce, 3 days after end
One year ago today: RomeEvery memberOnce, 1 year after start

Reminders go to the inbox, obey trip mute and the reminders switch, and skip the limits — they are few and deliberate. No marketing or win-back reminders in v1.

Inbox

  • A bell in the top bar, web and app, with the unread count.
  • Read when opened — on any device, stored on the server.
  • Tap opens the exact thing. One web URL the app also handles.
  • Kept 30 days, 50 per page.
  • Hidden when its thing is deleted or you left the trip.

Sending

A Railway cron runs every 5 minutes (Railway's floor): finds notifications past their wait, groups them, applies limits, sends. All state lives in the database, so a restart or a second web instance never loses or doubles a message.

  • A separate Railway cron service, schedule */5 * * * *, runs node floc/apps/web/scripts/cron-push.mjs. It only calls POST /api/cron/push with CRON_SECRET; the web service does the work.
  • Push waits 2 minutes. Each run claims its rows with a 5-minute lease, so an overlapping run skips them and a crashed send is retried.
  • A phone only gets notifications from after it registered.
  • Android push needs google-services.json (EAS file variable GOOGLE_SERVICES_JSON) and the FCM key uploaded to EAS.
