Competitor: Polarsteps

URL: https://www.polarsteps.com/
Positioning: "Plan, track & relive your trips" — a travel-tracking and memory app focused on automatic GPS route capture, photo journalling, and turning trips into shareable books and videos. 22M+ users.

Features

Trip tracking (core differentiator)

  • Automatic GPS route capture — records your path continuously in the background while you travel
  • Offline tracking — keeps tracking with no signal; battery-efficient (~4% per day)
  • Real-time location sharing — friends and family follow your trip live via a shared link
  • Trip stats — distance, countries, cities, time on the road auto-calculated

Planning

  • Itinerary builder — add stays and activities; see trip plotted on the map
  • AI Trip Planner — personalised travel tips integrated into the planner
  • Map-first view — full route visible across the whole trip

Journalling & memories

  • Photos and stories — add photos, videos, and written notes to each stop
  • Trip Reel — auto-generated shareable video of your trip with one button
  • Travel Books — order a professionally printed hardback photo book of your trip; giftable

Privacy & sharing

  • Full privacy control — trips are private by default; share selectively
  • Shareable link — share a live or completed trip without the other person needing an account
  • Ad-free — Polarsteps Plus subscription removes no ads (already ad-free by positioning)

Polarsteps Plus (paid)

  • Enhanced book and video features (implied; exact tier details not listed on homepage)

Gaps vs Floc

Gap Notes
No group planning / collaboration Sharing is one-to-many broadcast; no co-planning or joint editing
No expense tracking or splitting No money features at all
No recommendations or discovery Purely personal — no "what should I do here" layer
No booking / reservation management No email import, no integration with flights or hotels
No voting or group decision-making No social mechanics beyond following someone's trip
Minimal pre-trip planning Itinerary builder is basic; the product shines during and after a trip, not before

Strategic note: Polarsteps owns the "during and after" travel moment — automatic tracking, journalling, and keepsakes. Floc owns "before" (planning). These are largely complementary rather than directly competitive, but Polarsteps' 22M user base and the Travel Book monetisation model are worth noting. The risk is that Polarsteps expands its planning layer (it already has an AI planner); the opportunity is that Floc could surface post-trip memory/stats features to close the loop.
