# Competitor: Wanderlog

**URL:** https://wanderlog.com/  
**Positioning:** "One app for all your travel planning needs" — a full-stack itinerary planner with map-first design, AI assistance, route optimisation, and budget tracking.

---

## Features

### Planning & itinerary
- **Map + itinerary in one view** — no context-switching between map and list
- **Add places from guides with 1 click** — crawled web data pre-loaded; tap to add
- **Personalised recommendations** — top attractions and restaurants based on your itinerary
- **Road trip planner** — multi-stop routing with distance/time between each leg, best-stops lists
- **Route optimisation** — auto-rearranges stops for the most efficient order (Pro)
- **AI Assistant** — describe a trip and get a day-by-day itinerary; AI packing lists
- **Packing checklists** — preset or custom lists; to-do and shopping lists too
- **Scheduled visit times** — lock specific times to stops; see travel time between them

### Reservations & bookings
- **Email import** — forward confirmation emails to auto-populate flights, hotels, restaurants
- **Gmail auto-scan** — connect Gmail for automatic reservation sync (Pro)
- **Reservations view** — all bookings, attachments, and confirmation emails in one place
- **Unlimited attachments** — PDFs, files per trip (Pro)
- **Export to Google Maps** — push your itinerary places to Google Maps; auto-updates (Pro)

### Budget & expenses
- **Expense tracking** — log costs per stop or day
- **Cost splitting** — split expenses between trip members
- **Budget overview** — totals and breakdown by category

### Collaboration
- **Real-time collaborative editing** — live sync; multiple people editing simultaneously
- **Flight status tracking** — monitor flights in real time; push notifications (Pro)

### Offline & mobile
- **Offline access** — locally downloaded trip plans (Pro)
- **iOS and Android apps** — 4.9 App Store, 4.7 Google Play (Editor's Choice)
- **Browser extension** — save places from the web directly into a trip

### Discovery & social
- **User-shared guides** — browse community itineraries (e.g. "Paris 5-day", "Japan video game guide")
- **Guide library** — inspiration by destination; add any guide's stops to your own trip in 1 click
- **Flight deals** — cheap flight alerts to your inbox (Pro)

## Pricing

| Tier | Price | Key limits |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Core planning, map view, collaboration, email import, expense tracking |
| Pro | Annual only (no monthly option) | Offline access, route optimisation, Gmail auto-scan, flight status, unlimited attachments, export to Google Maps, flight deals |

*Note: users flag the lack of a monthly subscription option as a friction point.*

---

## Gaps vs Waypoint

| Gap | Notes |
|---|---|
| **No live travel / Trip Mode** | No offline "what's next" today-view while actually on the ground |
| **No Travel Passport / stats** | No visited-country tracking or cumulative travel stats |
| **No voting mechanic** | Real-time co-editing but no structured "vote on this activity" flow |
| **No public trip marketplace** | Guides are community-shared but no clone-and-customise trip flow |
| **No city safety/cost data layer** | Recommendations are crowdsourced; no structured city intel like Wanderlust |

**Strategic note:** Wanderlog is Waypoint's closest direct competitor — it covers planning, maps, collaboration, budgeting, and AI all in one. Its 8M+ trips planned and 4.9 App Store rating signal strong product-market fit. The key differentiation opportunity for Waypoint is the group-first experience: structured voting, cleaner split-money UX, and a tighter social layer around shared trips.
