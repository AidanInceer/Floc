# Competitor: TripIt

**URL:** https://www.tripit.com/  
**Positioning:** "The world's highest-rated travel organiser" — a trip-storage and itinerary-consolidation tool that builds a master itinerary from your booking confirmation emails.

---

## Features

### Core (free)
- **Email forwarding to itinerary** — forward any booking confirmation (flight, hotel, car, restaurant) and TripIt parses it into a structured itinerary automatically
- **Master itinerary** — all trips consolidated into a single timeline view
- **Calendar sync** — push trips to Google Calendar, Outlook, Apple Calendar
- **Sharing** — share a trip view with others (read-only link or traveller invite)
- **Offline access** — cached itinerary available without signal
- **Map view** — see itinerary stops on a map
- **Mobile apps** — iOS and Android

### Pro ($49/yr)
- **Real-time flight alerts** — gate changes, delays, cancellations pushed to your phone
- **Alternate flight suggestions** — if your flight is cancelled, TripIt Pro surfaces options
- **Seat tracking** — monitors seat inventory and alerts on upgrades
- **Points & miles tracker** — aggregates frequent flyer balances across programmes
- **Price drop alerts** — notifies if a booked flight drops in price
- **Check-in reminders** — automated 24-hour check-in nudge
- **Nearby airport alternatives** — suggests alternate airports on disruptions

## Gaps vs Waypoint

| Gap | Notes |
|---|---|
| **No planning** | TripIt organises what you've already booked — it never helps you decide where to go or what to do |
| **No collaboration / group planning** | Sharing is read-only; no co-editing, voting, or joint decision-making |
| **No expense splitting** | Tracks trip costs in aggregate but has no split-between-friends mechanic |
| **No AI or recommendations** | Purely reactive — ingests confirmations, never suggests |
| **No social/discovery layer** | No public trips, no community, no inspiration feed |
| **No day optimisation** | Displays the itinerary as booked; doesn't reorder or optimise stops |

**Strategic note:** TripIt's core moat is the email-parsing pipeline — travellers trust it as a passive inbox-to-itinerary converter. Waypoint does not yet import booking confirmations, which is a meaningful friction point once users have a booked trip they want to track.
