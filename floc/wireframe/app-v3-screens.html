7 · Profile Two groups, on purpose. The web splits what you curate from what you configure across two pages; a phone has one tab, so the split becomes two headings rather than two destinations.

Why the split works: Overview and Days divide a trip along a line that already exists in the data. Days come from day rows; people, places and files do not belong to any one date. So an undated trip is not a special case being handled — Overview is simply the half that still has something to show. No flag, no lifecycle state, nothing gated.
Still open: the sheet is a drawer, and drawers get ignored. Money is the section people reach for most, which is why it also sits in the header as a balance chip. If a second section turns out to need that escape hatch, the sheet is the wrong answer and this direction needs revisiting.
